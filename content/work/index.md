Through [O2A Labs](https://o2alabs.co.uk/), I work with early-stage founders and agencies that need experienced technical leadership — without hiring a full-time CTO.

## What I do

- **Fractional CTO** — Ongoing technical direction and leadership, typically two to four days a month.
- **Software architecture** — An independent look at your system, its constraints, and the decisions that matter next.
- **AI and software delivery** — Practical help designing and building software, including AI where it solves a real problem.
- **Technical due diligence** — Assessing a team, product, or codebase before you invest, acquire, or commit.
- **Engineering leadership** — Hiring, team structure, process, and mentoring for teams that need to grow without slowing down.

## Who I work with

> I’m usually the right fit for early-stage founders building a first product or team, and for agencies and consultancies that need architecture or delivery support on client work.

## How it works

1. **An intro call.** No cost, no pitch. We work out whether there’s a fit.
2. **Scoped work.** I propose a defined engagement: an assessment, a specific piece of delivery, or an ongoing arrangement.
3. **Ongoing involvement.** Usually two to four days a month, working directly with your team.

When a project needs more hands, I bring in a small group of trusted associates. I lead the work and stay accountable for it.

## Where I work

I’m based in the UK and work remotely with clients across the UK, the United States, and Europe.
